Frequently Asked Questions

Before You Get Started

What is included in a monthly digital marketing plan?

Deliverables depend on the selected service and agreed scope. The quotation should specify activities, reporting, timelines and what is not included.

Is the 6-month or 12-month package paid monthly?

The displayed 6-month and 12-month prices are total package fees. Payment milestones and whether instalments are available should be agreed in writing before the project starts.

Is Google Ads or Meta Ads budget included?

No. The displayed price is for campaign management. Advertising spend paid to Google or Meta is separate.

Do you guarantee first-page Google rankings?

No one can responsibly guarantee a specific Google ranking. SEO work focuses on improving technical quality, relevance, content and search visibility.

How much does website development cost?

Basic websites start at ₹15,000 for up to 5 pages, professional websites start at ₹25,000 for up to 10 pages, and e-commerce projects start at ₹35,000. Final pricing depends on scope.

How do I start working with you?

Send your business name, website if available, required service and main goal through the contact form. We can discuss the scope and prepare a quotation.

Are taxes and third-party tools included?

Taxes, hosting, domains, premium tools and other third-party expenses should be confirmed in the quotation. They are not automatically included in the listed starting prices.
